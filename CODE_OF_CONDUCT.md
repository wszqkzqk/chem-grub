# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Chemistry GRUB to be an open, welcoming, and technically focused
community. Participation should be respectful regardless of a person's
background, experience, identity, or chosen operating system.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing.
- Give useful reproduction details and assume good faith.
- Welcome new contributors and explain project conventions patiently.
- Respect privacy, consent, and other people's time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
doxxing, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not upload
malicious boot configurations or request credentials, private keys, or other
sensitive information.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces and to public representation of the
project, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and review comments.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through GitHub Issues at
https://github.com/wszqkzqk/chem-grub/issues. Project maintainers will review
reports fairly and may remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts
when necessary to keep the community safe. Do not include private information
in a public report; ask for a private contact route instead.
